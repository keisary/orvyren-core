Earlier this week KPK proactively patched a potential issue in the underlying Roles Modifier framework. Our assessment confirmed no impact on the endowment, and no action is required on the side of ENS. Even without the patch, the Avatar Safe permission framework would have prevented the issue from resulting in any loss of funds.

We are referring all information to the [posts done by the Zodiac team](https://x.com/zodiaceco/status/2061862711206502902?s=20) at this time.
