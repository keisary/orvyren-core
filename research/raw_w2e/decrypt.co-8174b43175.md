**Zurich, Switzerland, March 18th, 2025, Chainwire**

[Brahma](https://brahma.fi/) is launching **ConsoleKit**, a full-stack infrastructure designed to streamline on-chain automation with enhanced security and efficiency. Onchain automation has long been fragmented, requiring manual oversight and disconnected tooling. With over $1 billion in transactions across multiple chains, Brahma is demonstrating that true autonomous execution is now a reality.

Building on its innovations with Automated Vaults and Accounts, ConsoleKit enables automation directly from user-controlled accounts, with robust policy management ensuring all agent actions remain secure and self-custodial—eliminating the need for manual oversight.

**Onchain Without Limits**

Now live with Tempest, Creator.bid, and Swell, and integrated with Morpho, Agentic Protocols function as self-sustaining on-chain agents that execute transactions, manage assets, and optimize operations directly from user-controlled accounts. Unlike traditional smart contracts or centralized services, they operate continuously with custom logic and decentralized execution, while policy guardrails ensure all actions remain secure and self-custodial. Powered by AI-driven reasoning and Web2 integrations, these agents—enabled by Brahma ConsoleKit—support real-world use cases such as automated payroll, treasury management, operational fund allocation, and digital asset-based financial solutions.

**These Agentic Protocols enable:**

- 24/7 execution across multiple chains without manual intervention
- Automated portfolio management through rebalancing and compounding mechanisms.
- Integration with Web2 data sources for risk management and financial operations
- Coordination across multiple protocols to maximize efficiency
- Full user custody through secure, programmable execution with policy guardrails that confine agent actions.

Upcoming integrations with protocols on Berachain, BNB Chain, Base, and more will extend agentic execution into new frontiers, making on-chain interactions more seamless than ever. By bridging Web2 and Web3, Agentic Protocols unlock a future where on-chain runs trustlessly, efficiently, and without limits.

**The Infrastructure Powering Onchain Autonomy**

> “The age of passive, manual onchain transactions is over. Intelligent, self-sustaining agents will define the next decade,” said Alessandro Tenconi, CEO of Brahma. “Agentic Protocols aren’t just about automation, they represent an entirely new paradigm where strategies run independently, adapting to market conditions with zero friction.”

[Brahma’s](https://brahma.fi/?utm_source=Decrypt&utm_medium=PR&utm_campaign=Agentic+Protocol) execution stack, policy management, and automation framework provide the backbone for Agentic Protocols, enabling developers to:

- Deploy AI-driven strategies with deterministic execution flows.
- Automate onchain financial workflows, including payments, asset swapping, liquidity provisioning, and portfolio rebalancing and risk management.
- Enable interoperable automation across multiple protocols and networks.
- Connect onchain & Web2 services, to let agents perform traditional services too, while being able to spend onchain as well, expanding real-world utility.

Built with a modular and secure architecture, these agents ensure gas efficiency, reliable execution, and trustless enforcement of security policies, unlocking a new era where on-chain agents operate autonomously.

**A Paradigm Shift in Agentic Protocols**

> “Onchain agents with powerful capabilities aren’t coming, they are here,” added Kakusan, Co-founder of Brahma. “We’re seeing protocols launch agents that execute strategies, manage assets, and bridge ecosystems in ways never seen before. With more agents on Berachain, and upcoming integration with teams like GlueX, and with other agents releasing soon, we’re just scratching the surface of what’s possible.”

Brahma’s vision isn’t just about better automation, it’s about making onchain actors truly self-sustaining. With Brahma’s agentic infrastructure, developers, DAOs, and protocols can build self-sustaining Agentic Protocols today.

Users can already build, deploy, and optimize with [Brahma ConsoleKit](https://brahma.fi/console-kit?utm_source=Decrypt&utm_medium=PR&utm_campaign=Agentic+Protocol)

Users can learn more about ConsoleKit [here](https://brahma.fi/blog/introducing-agentic-protocols?utm_source=Decrypt&utm_medium=PR&utm_campaign=Agentic+Protocol).

For the latest agentic updates, users can follow [@BrahmaFi](https://x.com/BrahmaFi?utm_source=Decrypt&utm_medium=PR&utm_campaign=Agentic+Protocol) on Twitter.
