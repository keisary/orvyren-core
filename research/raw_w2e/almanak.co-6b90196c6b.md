04Custody

## Your capital never leaves _your control_

Your funds stay in your Safe. Zodiac Roles gives each strategy only the permissions it needs - and keeps everything else off limits.

**Safe** custody. **Zodiac**-enforced permissions.

![Safe](https://almanak.co/assets/safe-logo-hd.png?dpl=dpl_Hmk9bc3CVbkqzZGpRFXkH56KLf1H)+![Zodiac](https://almanak.co/assets/zodiac-wordmark.svg?dpl=dpl_Hmk9bc3CVbkqzZGpRFXkH56KLf1H)

### Non-custodial

Your assets stay in your Safe smart account. Almanak never takes custody and cannot move funds beyond the permissions you approve.

### Scoped permissions

Zodiac Roles restrict each strategy to approved contracts, actions, and spending limits. Revoke access at any time.

### Gateway architecture

Strategy containers run with no secrets and no internet access. Every external call is mediated through a controlled gateway sidecar.

### Verifiable

Every strategy is real, auditable code you own - versioned in your own GitHub repo. Simulate and stress-test on mainnet forks before you go live.
