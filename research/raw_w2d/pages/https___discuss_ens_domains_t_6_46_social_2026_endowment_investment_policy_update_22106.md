# [Heading link](https://discuss.ens.domains/t/6-46-social-2026-endowment-investment-policy-update/22106\#p-61374-about-this-proposal-1) About this Proposal

This proposal is an update to the ENS Endowment Investment Policy Statement. It revises the framework governing the management of the ENS Endowment Fund by KPK on behalf of ENS DAO, updating the investment objectives, risk parameters, constraints, and governance mechanisms within which the Endowment Manager operates.

Upon passing snapshot, it supersedes the [previous Investment Policy Statement](https://copper-added-anglerfish-892.mypinata.cloud/ipfs/bafybeiajihjdrplt75h36upclptjmkqziboekuf7e25fgfuyk2m54sonfi) and serves as the primary reference for investment guidance pertaining to the ENS Endowment.

#### [Heading link](https://discuss.ens.domains/t/6-46-social-2026-endowment-investment-policy-update/22106\#p-61374-for-delegates-2) For Delegates:

- A summary of changes from 2025 is listed [here](https://discuss.ens.domains/t/temp-check-2026-endowment-investment-policy-update/22106/2).
- The ratified version will be pinned to IPFS and shared with the DAO on the governance forum for record keeping.

**Update:** This has passed social vote. A copy of the 2026 IPS is permalinked/pinned here: [https://tomato-careful-otter-747.mypinata.cloud/ipfs/bafybeigwpq44fxgxjgst7yskemkawcb7v4leh3pez5e23gsoox55b6mpa4](https://tomato-careful-otter-747.mypinata.cloud/ipfs/bafybeigwpq44fxgxjgst7yskemkawcb7v4leh3pez5e23gsoox55b6mpa4)

## [Heading link](https://discuss.ens.domains/t/6-46-social-2026-endowment-investment-policy-update/22106\#p-61374-h-1-introduction-4) 1\. Introduction

This Investment Policy Statement (‘IPS’) governs the management of the ENS Endowment Fund (the ‘Endowment’) by the appointed Endowment Manager on behalf of ENS DAO. It defines the investment objectives, risk parameters, constraints, and governance mechanisms within which the Endowment Manager operates, and serves as the primary reference for investment governance, financial planning, and accountability.
