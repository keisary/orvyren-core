### How pricing works

1. 1


#### Each request uses compute units



Different API methods use different numbers of CUs. If you estimate from requests, choose an average that reflects the methods your app calls.

2. 2


#### Flat-rate compute pricing



Compute usage costs $0.525 per 1 million CUs. Throughput capacity is priced separately.

3. 3


#### Peak capacity is separate



Pay as You Go includes 300 RPS (10,000 CU/s). Above that, each additional 5,000 CU/s block costs $160 per month.

4. 4


#### Self-serve capacity ends at 30,000 CU/s



If you need more than 30,000 CU/s, contact sales for a custom capacity quote.

- What blockchains and networks does Alchemy support?







Alchemy supports all major Layer 1 and Layer 2 networks including Ethereum, Solana, BNB Smart Chain, Avalanche, Polygon PoS, Arbitrum, Optimism, Base, Starknet, ZKsync, Blast, and many more. Visit our [documentation](https://docs.alchemy.com/) for the complete list of supported chains.

- How does invoicing and billing work?







For Pay As You Go plans, you'll be billed monthly based on your actual usage. You can view your current usage and estimated costs in the [Alchemy Dashboard](https://dashboard.alchemy.com/settings/billing) at any time. Enterprise customers receive custom invoicing based on their contract terms.

- Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?







Yes! You can upgrade from Free to Pay As You Go at any time directly from your dashboard. For Enterprise plans, contact our sales team. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

- What do key terms like CU, throughput, and SLA mean?







CU (Compute Units): A measure of computational resource usage. Throughput: The maximum number of requests per second your plan supports. SLA (Service Level Agreement): Guaranteed uptime and response time commitments. Rate Limit: The maximum number of API requests allowed per second.
